About

A feast built for sharing

Mezban is how Chattogram feeds a crowd: big pots, slow fire and a beef curry full of whole spices. Mezbani Adda brings that table to Mohammadpur, so you don't need a wedding invitation to eat it.

Slow-cooked

Mezbani beef takes its time, and it tastes like it.

Made for groups

Bring the whole adda. Mezban was always meant to be shared.

Open late

Until 11pm, every day of the week.
